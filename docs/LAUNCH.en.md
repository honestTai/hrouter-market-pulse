# Introducing Hrouter Market Pulse

An open-source market research workbench for A-shares, Hong Kong, and US equities, available as a standalone local dashboard and a Codex plugin.

![English workbench with synthetic demonstration data](images/dashboard-en.png)

The workbench brings quotes, holdings, financial charts, indicators, evidence and decision records together. Chinese and English interfaces share the same data. Quote time and report time remain separate, indicator periods are explicit, and missing or conflicting data stays visible.

Stateful alerts track meaningful transitions instead of repeatedly reporting an unchanged condition. Research records preserve original sources and first-observed availability. A decision journal captures the thesis before an outcome and appends later reviews.

Deterministic tools calculate the numbers while the current Codex task model handles research and interpretation. Shared-capital backtests and purged walk-forward evaluations expose their assumptions. Model qualification is a check in the program, not an adjective in a report.

The repository includes the installable plugin, a synthetic demo, source code, tests and bilingual documentation. Public data can be delayed or unavailable; coverage and execution assumptions are documented. There is no order-execution integration or promise of returns.
